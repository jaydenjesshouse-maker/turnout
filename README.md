# Turnout

Jayden's wardrobe app. This page is the app on your Home Screen; your wear log, plans and new photos live in a private Google Sheet and Drive folder, reached through Apps Script.

- `index.html` the app (paste the Apps Script link where it says PASTE)
- `wardrobe.json` your clothes; `img/` their studio shots and your own photos
- `manifest.json`, `icon-*.png`, `sw.js` the icon, and the copy kept on your phone
